# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 22, 2026_

Qualifying: Lap Timer does not collect, sell or share any personal data.

- **No accounts and no tracking.** The developer runs no analytics of its own.
- **One app recommendation for free users.** On iPhone and iPad, Settings
  shows one card from [Kickstart Exchange](https://exchange.kickstart.tools),
  a cross-promotion service for independent apps. It is hidden with
  Qualifying Pro. To show the card, the app sends Kickstart Exchange
  app-level details: the app's key, bundle identifier, platform, app and
  build version, SDK version, App Store country, and Apple's signed proof
  that the app came from the App Store. Kickstart Exchange records which card
  was shown, whether it was seen, and whether it was tapped or reported. These
  records are kept for about 30 days, are not linked to you or your device,
  and are not used to track you. See the
  [Kickstart Exchange privacy notice](https://exchange.kickstart.tools/privacy).
- **Your sessions stay yours.** Sessions, laps, notes and habits are stored on
  your device and, if iCloud is on, in your private iCloud database so your own
  devices stay in sync. The developer cannot read that data.
- **Notifications** are scheduled locally on your device to tell you when a
  countdown reaches its final minutes or ends.

If you delete the app, its local data is removed. iCloud data can be removed in
Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/ShawnBaek/qualifying-app/issues.
