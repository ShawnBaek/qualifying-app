# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 29, 2026_

Qualifying: Lap Timer does not collect, sell or share any personal data.

- **No accounts, analytics, ads or tracking.** The app contains no third-party SDKs.
- **Your sessions stay yours.** Sessions, laps, notes and habits are stored on
  your device and, if iCloud is on, in your private iCloud database so your own
  devices stay in sync. The developer cannot read that data.
- **Notifications** are scheduled locally on your device to tell you when a
  countdown reaches its final minutes or ends.

If you delete the app, its local data is removed. iCloud data can be removed in
Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/ShawnBaek/qualifying-app/issues.
