# Support

Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue:
https://github.com/ShawnBaek/qualifying-app/issues

**Sync not working?** Make sure every device is signed in to the same Apple
Account with iCloud Drive turned on, then reopen the app.

**No beep or vibration?** Check Settings › Highlight & alerts › Sound and
Haptics in the app, and the ring/silent switch on iPhone.
